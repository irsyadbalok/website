## Data this site collects

This site has no forms, user accounts or tracking cookies. You can read every page without giving any personal data.

## Links to other systems

Pupil registration, the parent portal and the staff portal are run by the school management system at `my21.awfatech.com`. Data you enter there is covered by that system’s privacy notice, not this one.

## Photographs of pupils

Photographs of pupils are published only with the consent of a parent or guardian. A pupil’s full name, class and face are not combined in one caption. If you want a photograph taken down, contact the school office and it will be removed promptly.

## Contact us

Questions about personal data can be sent to the school office using the details on the Contact page.
