## Vision

To lead a comprehensive, outstanding and respected system of Islamic education that shapes Rabbani leaders and a Rabbani community.

## Mission

1. To build a network of educational services as an extension of Islamic education.
2. To run an effective and innovative management system.
3. To keep a committed body of educators.
4. To keep offering education packages that are attractive and affordable.
5. To provide complete facilities and a conducive environment for shaping Rabbani individuals who can contribute to the ummah.

## Motto

> Ke Arah Pembentukan Insan Rabbani

In English: towards the formation of the Rabbani individual.
