It is a process founded on the creed of tauhid, to build and bring forth Rabbani individuals who are knowledgeable, faithful and of noble character, and who are able to shape their own lives, society, the nation and the world as required by the Quran and the Sunnah, through a system that is balanced, integrated, comprehensive and continuous.
