## Mission

To bring forth pupils of noble character, who are well-mannered and set a good example.

## Vision

- To instil spiritual values in pupils
- To encourage pupils to show their talents in every field
- To create a harmonious school atmosphere
- To reduce discipline problems among pupils

## Motto

> Tarbiyah asas pembentukan sahsiah

In English: tarbiyah is the foundation of character. Discipline is not merely scolding; it is firmness together with love in educating.

## Areas of responsibility

- Discipline
- Al-Quran
- Pre-tahfiz
- Spiritual life
- Surau and Imam Muda
- School broadcasts (siar raya)
