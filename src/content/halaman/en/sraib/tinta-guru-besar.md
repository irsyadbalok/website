Praise be to Allah for His countless blessings, and for the life still lent to us to carry on the trust placed upon us on this earth. Peace and blessings be upon the Prophet Muhammad SAW, his family and his companions.

Every beginning has its end. Every end has a new beginning, for as long as life in this world goes on. A world that builds its physical side ever more greedily, without regard for human development, raises a new generation that is high in intellect and poor in manners. Seeing this gap, educational institutions have the greatest part to play in closing it.

In 2026, Sekolah Rendah Islam Al-Irsyad Balok (SRAIB) places its focus on “awwaludiin ma’rifatullah”. Once the SRAIB community knows its Creator, good manners will take shape alongside a sharp mind, producing khalifah who make the earth prosper in the mould set by Allah SWT. This task is very large and heavy to achieve alone. To reach this aspiration, let us all work shoulder to shoulder, cooperate, unite our hearts and help one another to realise the dream of SRAIB 2026.

May every effort and commitment be rewarded as continuing charity, in the meaning of the words of the Messenger of Allah SAW:

> When a child of Adam dies, his deeds come to an end except for three: continuing charity, beneficial knowledge, or the prayer of a righteous child.

Wallahua’lam.

Yours sincerely,

**Salmah binti Mat Yusoff**  
Headmistress
