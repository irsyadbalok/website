## Main academic focus for 2026

- Quality of teaching and learning (PdP)
- Checking of pupils’ work
- Intensive Jawi classes
- School work and homework set for pupils
- Pupil excellence: mastery of every subject
- Stronger Classroom Assessment (PBD), with a minimum Mastery Level of 4
- A revived NILAM programme: encouraging pupils to read one book a day

## Subjects

Each subject is managed by a subject panel:

- Bahasa Melayu
- English
- Mathematics
- Science
- Arabic
- Islamic Education
- Physical and Health Education
- Design and Technology
- History
- KAFA

## Assessment and examinations

- Classroom Assessment (PBD)
- UPSA and UASA, with answering-technique workshops
- UPKK, with answering-technique workshops and a solat hajat programme in preparation
- A ceremony to announce major examination results
