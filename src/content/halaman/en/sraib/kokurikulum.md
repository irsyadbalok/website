## Direction

**Vision.** Sekolah Rendah Islam Al-Irsyad Balok among the schools that excel in co-curriculum at zone, district and state level.

**Mission.** To educate, train and bring forth pupils who excel in co-curriculum: athletes who are fit, minds that are sharp and character that is praiseworthy.

**Goal.** To strive for all-round pupil success and excellence in co-curriculum, complementing success in academics and character, in line with the Philosophy of Islamic Education and the National Philosophy of Education.

**Motto.** Berilmu, berdisiplin dan berbakti (knowledgeable, disciplined and of service).

## Co-curriculum charter

- Every pupil must join one uniformed unit, one club or society, and one sport or game.
- Every pupil is given the chance to join co-curricular activities according to interest and ability, subject to quotas and vacancies.
- Every pupil is given the chance to take part at zone, district, state, national and international level.
- Every pupil takes part in co-curricular activities under safety insurance cover.
- Every pupil who takes part must follow instructions and rules.
- All pupils and coordinating teachers must give priority to personal safety.
- Pupils may not carry out co-curricular activities without the supervision of a coordinating teacher.

## Objectives

- To develop pupils’ talents and interests in activities outside the classroom.
- To bring forth pupils who are proactive and community-minded.
- To build a strong competitive and fighting spirit, up to more demanding levels.
- To bring forth individuals with a firm identity who are dedicated, capable, caring, tolerant and responsible.
- To give pupils the chance to gain knowledge, experience, basic skills, and the ability to manage, lead and be independent.
- To enable more of the school’s participants to represent the district, state and country in co-curricular work.
- To make pupils individuals who are always innovative and creative in any task.
- To make the school atmosphere more attractive and enjoyable for pupils, in the effort to build an outstanding school culture.
