We, the community of Sekolah Rendah Islam Al-Irsyad Balok, are committed to the following:

1. Establishing a foundation of understanding of Islam.
2. Creating a righteous environment (biah solehah).
3. Developing personal potential in:
   - knowledge
   - faith
   - character
   - family life
   - deeds
4. Excellent achievement in major and internal examinations.
