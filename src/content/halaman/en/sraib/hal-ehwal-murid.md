## Vision

To bring forth pupils of high character, with a positive attitude, who excel in every field in this world and the hereafter.

## Mission

To equip pupils with a range of knowledge and skills that strengthen their character, so that they are highly motivated, hold noble ambitions and have undivided determination to achieve excellence in this world and the hereafter.

## Objectives

- To bring forth pupils who are capable and competitive, so that they succeed inside and outside school.
- To nurture love and care for oneself, family, school, society and the environment.
- To instil a firm identity and a solid sense of trustworthiness in pupils.
- To train pupils who can lead and can readily be led, wherever they are.
- To strengthen good values and noble character through programmes organised by the school.

## Client charter of the Student Affairs Unit

We, the teachers of SRI Al-Irsyad Balok, with full awareness and dedication, will ensure for all pupils:

1. Noble character, wisdom and ability in the physical, emotional, spiritual and intellectual domains.
2. Fair affection for every pupil, by putting pupils’ safety and welfare first.
3. Leadership qualities in each pupil, so that they become capable leaders.
4. A school that is safe and conducive for all pupils and the whole community.
5. Close cooperation among pupils, teachers, school staff and the local community in many matters.
6. A warm relationship with pupils’ parents and guardians.
7. A spirit of consensus with the surrounding community.
8. A quality service to all parties.
9. Environmentally friendly and green management of the school surroundings.
