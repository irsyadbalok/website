Sekolah Rendah Islam Al-Irsyad Balok (SRAIB) was founded in January 2000 on the proposal of Ustaz Soleh bin Senafi. The proposal was welcomed by Doktor Syed Badaruddin bin Syed Ali, Tuan Haji Faiz bin Baharuddin, Tuan Haji Khasbullah bin Kader and several others.

SRAIB was born of the local community’s awareness of, and concern for, Islamic-based education in the upbringing of children. The school was registered with the Pahang Islamic Religious Department under registration number KS 0175/03, dated 21 January 2008.

SRAIB began operating with 25 pupils and 3 teachers, in a rented house near the surau of Kampung Chengal Lempong.

In its second year, SRAIB received a waqf donation of a building with 6 classrooms on the lot at 16/1224 Kampung Chengal Lempong. The following year, SRAIB obtained a three-acre plot of land, given as waqf by two benefactors through the Pahang Islamic Religious and Malay Customs Council (MAIP).

As of 2021, SRAIB had 24 classes and 868 pupils. Its staff consisted of 66 academic staff, one clerk and two general assistants. Its facilities included an administrative office, a briefing room, a staff room, a canteen, a library, a science room, a Design and Technology (RBT) workshop, an open hall, a school shop, a library hall, a Living Skills (KH) room, a Home Economics (ERT) room, study huts and an ICT lab with 12 computers for pupils’ use.

SRAIB greatly needs the firm support of many parties to keep growing and flourishing as a place to educate children and instil in them the values of Islam, so that they carry on a generation of the muttaqin.
