## Name

The school is named Al-Irsyad, which means guidance. The shape of the logo is likened to a tree, in keeping with the words of Allah S.W.T., which mean:

> It yields its fruit in every season by the permission of its Lord. Allah S.W.T. sets forth parables for people so that they may always remember. (Surah Ibrahim, verse 25)

## Shapes

Each part of the logo carries a meaning:

- Kubah Islamik (the Islamic dome)
- Gerbang Jalan Kejayaan (the gateway on the road to success)
- Laksana Islam Keseluruhan (Islam practised in full)
- Kalam (the pen)
- Amal (deeds)
- Ilmu Al-Quran (knowledge of the Quran)
- Iman (faith)
